How to convert from Celsius (°C) to Fahrenheit (°F)

This website convert Celsius to Fahrenheit and vice versa using a standard formulation of conversion.
T in Fahrenheit (°F) can be converted into degrees Celsius (°C) through the following calculation:

T (°F) = (T(°C) x 9/5) + 32
or
T(°F) = (T(°C) x 1.8) + 32
